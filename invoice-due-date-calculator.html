How invoice due dates work

Payment terms tell your client when an invoice should be paid. For example, Net 30 normally means payment is due 30 calendar days after the invoice date, unless your agreement says the payment clock starts from another event.

Common payment terms

  • Due on receipt: payment is due immediately.
  • Net 7: payment is due 7 days after the invoice date.
  • Net 15: payment is due 15 days after the invoice date.
  • Net 30: payment is due 30 days after the invoice date.
  • Net 60: payment is due 60 days after the invoice date.
  • Net 90: payment is due 90 days after the invoice date.

This is a planning calculator. Your contract, customer terms and applicable local rules determine the actual enforceable payment deadline.
