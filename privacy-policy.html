Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 26, 2026

GenFreeInvoice ("we", "our", or "the site") respects your privacy. This policy explains what information we collect and how we use it.

1. Information We Do Not Collect

Our tools (invoice generator, calculators, contract generator) run entirely in your browser. Any names, amounts, or figures you enter are processed on your own device and are never transmitted to or stored on our servers.

2. Information We Automatically Collect

Like most websites, we may collect standard technical information automatically:

  • Browser type and device information
  • Pages visited and time spent on pages
  • Referring website (how you found us)
  • Approximate geographic region (country/city level, not exact location)

This information is used only to understand how the site is used and to improve our tools.

3. Cookies and Advertising

We may use third-party advertising services such as Google AdSense. These services may use cookies to show ads based on your prior visits to this or other websites. You can opt out of personalised advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings. You can also disable cookies in your browser at any time.

4. Third-Party Links

Our guides may contain links to external websites. We are not responsible for the privacy practices of other sites.

5. Data Security

Because your tool inputs never leave your device, the most sensitive data on this site is under your control at all times.

6. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated date.

7. Contact

For any privacy-related questions, contact us through the contact page.
