About GenFreeInvoice

Free invoice, pricing, tax-planning and money-management tools for freelancers and small businesses worldwide.

Why GenFreeInvoice exists

Freelancers and small businesses often need to prepare invoices, estimate project prices, plan savings and understand basic money questions without paying for another subscription. GenFreeInvoice brings practical browser-based tools and plain-language guides together in one place.

Our goal is to make routine finance tasks easier to understand. The tools provide calculations and planning estimates; they do not replace professional accounting, tax or legal advice.

Our principles

  • Accessible tools: core tools are free to use without creating an account.
  • Clear limitations: estimates should be labelled and interpreted according to their assumptions. For example, the tax estimator is designed for US federal tax planning, not every country.
  • Privacy-conscious design: tool inputs are intended to be processed in your browser. Contact-form submissions are different and are sent through the form service used on that page.
  • Useful explanations: guides aim to explain calculations and common decisions in straightforward language.
