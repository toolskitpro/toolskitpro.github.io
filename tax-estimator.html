What this calculator estimates

Enter your expected business revenue and deductible business expenses. The calculator estimates net business profit, self-employment tax, a simplified federal income tax amount, total estimated federal tax, and one quarter of that estimate as a budgeting guide.

How self-employment tax is calculated

For many self-employed people, net earnings from self-employment are generally multiplied by 92.35% to determine the amount used for self-employment tax. The basic rate combines 12.4% Social Security tax and 2.9% Medicare tax. Social Security tax is subject to an annual wage base, and other rules may apply, including Additional Medicare Tax for some taxpayers. This tool uses a simplified calculation and may not match your return exactly.

2026 income tax assumptions

The income-tax estimate uses the 2026 federal tax brackets for single filers or married couples filing jointly and the 2026 standard deduction. It does not support head-of-household or married-filing-separately status. It also omits some items that can change taxable income, including the qualified business income deduction, tax credits, other income, itemized deductions, and certain additional taxes. Your actual taxable income may be different.

Example: plan for a freelance year

Suppose you expect $60,000 in business revenue and $8,000 in deductible expenses. Your starting net profit is $52,000 before considering other tax adjustments. Keep invoices, receipts, mileage records where relevant, and statements in an organized place so you can update your estimate as income or costs change.

When should freelancers pay estimated tax?

The US federal tax system generally expects tax to be paid as income is earned, through withholding or estimated payments. Self-employed people often use quarterly estimated payments, but whether you need to pay and how much depends on your full tax situation. Use the IRS Form 1040-ES and Publication 505 guidance rather than relying only on dividing this calculator’s result by four.

Frequently asked questions

Is this calculator for the 2025 or 2026 tax year?

It uses 2026 federal income-tax brackets and standard deduction assumptions for planning 2026 income. Do not use it to calculate a 2025 tax return.

Does the estimate include state taxes?

No. It estimates selected US federal taxes only. State and local taxes are not included.

Is self-employment tax the same as income tax?

No. Self-employment tax mainly covers Social Security and Medicare. Federal income tax is separate, and eligible people may owe both.

Can I use the quarterly amount as my exact payment?

No. It is a rough budgeting figure. Your required estimated payments can depend on prior-year tax, withholding, credits, income timing, and other rules. Check Form 1040-ES or ask a tax professional.

Does this include every deduction and credit?

No. This simplified tool does not model every deduction, credit, special tax, or individual circumstance.
